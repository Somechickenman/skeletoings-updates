Update 0.3.0
- NEW: two bosses and two new maps you have to earn. Abominations on the Graveyard sometimes drop a soul key. It opens the mausoleum, and stairs lead down into the Ancient Mausoleum.
- NEW BOSS: the Ancient Homunculus. Ring the four bells in the tunnels and a huge mass of fused skeletons climbs out of the bone pit. Break its arms and skulls to reach its core. It drags you into itself (mash Space, or have a friend break the arm), raises homunculi, breathes bone-cracking gas, and grows as it absorbs bones.
- NEW MAP: the Catacombs, unlocked by beating the Ancient Homunculus. A closed stone maze with chests, crawlspaces and a sealed tomb.
- NEW BOSS: the Elder Slayer, asleep in the Catacombs' tomb. Find the soul key in a chest, wake him, and break through his armour. Beat him and the first to grab his fallen helm keeps it... though nobody can wear it yet.
- NEW: unlocks are your own. In a party the host picks from the maps they have earned.
- NEW: the Extra Skull, a little head on your shoulder that looks around, earned from the Ancient Homunculus. There's a secret for those who beat it.
- NEW: swords got heavy. Draw and sheathe on the hip (R + M1 / M2), heavy slashes, a cross slash with two swords, a spin with one. The new Shogun Hat turns any sword you hold into a katana.
- NEW: drink a cursed jar (Q) and keep what was inside. Tap Q to spit one out to hunt someone, Q + M1 / M2 to spit it into your hand. Die with them inside and they burst out at your killer.
- NEW: chests, cursed jars, crawlspaces, and joining a public party mid-match as a spectator.
- NEW: unique names (nobody else can take yours) and leaderboards for wins and time played. Turn on "Show me on leaderboards" in Settings to appear on them.
- Smarter AI: NPCs throw bones, books and lit bombs, ride fireworks, man the cannons and block then counter, depending on difficulty.
- Controls: R alone wears hats and armour and opens chests, F alone blocks, and only the prompt nearest the middle of your screen works.
- Real lanterns and torches on every map, with flickering flames, all hanging from something now.
- The Graveyard's mausoleum got a proper roof.
- Skulls have their brow above the eyes now.
- Hit freezes are gone, the air sledgehammer can only go downward after the first one, and legs are much harder to lose by tripping.

Update 0.2.0
- NEW MAP: the Castle. An indoor hall and an outdoor courtyard joined by doorways.
- Swords (one hand, long reach, knock bones loose easily but barely crush them) in the hall and the courtyard.
- Shields (one hand). Hold F + M1/M2 to block: the shield soaks the hit, the arm takes no damage. Shields wear out and break.
- Firework stand in the courtyard (new rockets appear on a timer). F near one, or while holding one, lights a 3 second fuse. R + M1/M2 grabs it, M1 + M2 grips it with both hands.
- Ride a rocket: hold it when the fuse ends and it carries you where you point for 6 seconds, then explodes. R lets go mid-flight. A rocket nobody holds shoots off in a straight line and explodes on the first thing it hits.
- Pirate ship: cannonballs are the bombs now. A landed cannonball sits on the deck as an inert bomb. Pick it up (R) to light a 4 second fuse; throw it or drop it, the fuse keeps burning. Sparking fuse, flashing bomb and a shrinking ring show the countdown. The old loose bombs are gone.
- Homunculi are cursed now: wrong sockets, wrong proportions, an arm where the head should be, twitchy jerky movement and a faint sickly green glow.
- Homunculi come sooner: crawlers 1:00, stumblers 1:45, bone piles 2:30, twin-skulls 3:15, first Abomination 4:00 and one more every minute.
- Legs are tougher: 50% more detach resistance and 3 crush HP.
- Crawling: legless skeletons can no longer jump. Hold Space for 2.5 seconds to scoop up a dirt ball, then throw it like a limb. It staggers whoever it hits.
- Online play fixes: clearer error messages, a 60 second wait for the sleeping server, a connection log (Open log folder on the Join screen), and a fix for the freezes and crashes when closing the game.
- New kill feed lines for fireworks, bombs, swords and dirt balls.
