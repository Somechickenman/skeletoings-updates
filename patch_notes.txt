Update 0.2.6
- NEW: daily quests. Three new quests every day, with a bonus for finishing all three. The first time you open the Quests tab each day, the cards are dealt out. A "!" shows on the Teeth & Trophies button and on the Shop and Quests tabs when there are new crates or quests.
- NEW: achievement unlocks are an event now. Banners and medals in bronze, silver, gold and secret, with big and secret ones getting a bigger show and their own jingle. Click an achievement to open its card and replay the unlock. New Settings: Unlock jingles volume, Reduce flashes and Show progress pops.
- NEW: Discord Rich Presence. Your Discord shows Playing Skeletoings with how long you have been playing, and whether you are in the menus, the sandbox or a match (map and player count, never a room code). There is a Show on Discord switch in Settings.
- NEW: F11 toggles fullscreen.
- NEW: a new game icon.
- Better explosions: bombs and cannonballs now have a flash, a fireball, sparks and rising smoke. Fireworks leave a colourful trail and burst into coloured shells with glitter.
- Fairer wins: if the other player leaves, the round is a no contest and nobody wins. A win now has to be earned with a kill or by lasting 30 seconds before it pays out Teeth, achievements or quests.
- It's Flyin' Time now counts properly, legs are a bit tougher, the shopkeeper talks in little blips and the digging sound is a real sound effect.
- The Settings page scrolls.

Update 0.2.5
- NEW: the Library is now a trap. Stand under a chandelier and it creaks, then drops two seconds later, crushing whatever is under it. A real ceiling with beams and a moonlit observatory window closes the room. Books on the tables are visible again and hit like a crushing sword.
- New achievements: It's Flyin' Time (be in the air for more than 10 seconds) and Glad You Like the Game (play for 1 hour).
- Achievements renamed and changed: Fabric Filcher (wear 5 different hats in one match), Breakdancer (secret: every emote on every map), Dung Beetle (knock a limb off with a dirt ball) and Nice Catch (block an unmanned firework with a shield).
- Smarter AI: NPCs grab swords, shields and books, run from creaking chandeliers, and lean toward fighting players. Homunculi always hunt players, never each other.
- The slow-motion death only happens when a player dies, not an NPC.
- Hosts get Retry round (and Back to lobby) on the round end screen, the death screen and the pause menu, so you don't have to go back to the main menu.
- Fixed grabbing a rocket with your free hand when the other hand already holds it.
- Fixed hats losing their skin when they fall off, and when someone picks one up.
- Fixed the achievement popup showing half cut off the screen.

Update 0.2.0
- NEW MAP: the Castle. An indoor hall and an outdoor courtyard joined by doorways.
- Swords (one hand, long reach, knock bones loose easily but barely crush them) in the hall and the courtyard.
- Shields (one hand). Hold F + M1/M2 to block: the shield soaks the hit, the arm takes no damage. Shields wear out and break.
- Firework stand in the courtyard (new rockets appear on a timer). F near one, or while holding one, lights a 3 second fuse. R + M1/M2 grabs it, M1 + M2 grips it with both hands.
- Ride a rocket: hold it when the fuse ends and it carries you where you point for 6 seconds, then explodes. R lets go mid-flight. A rocket nobody holds shoots off in a straight line and explodes on the first thing it hits.
- Pirate ship: cannonballs are the bombs now. A landed cannonball sits on the deck as an inert bomb. Pick it up (R) to light a 4 second fuse; throw it or drop it, the fuse keeps burning. Sparking fuse, flashing bomb and a shrinking ring show the countdown. The old loose bombs are gone.
- Homunculi are cursed now: wrong sockets, wrong proportions, an arm where the head should be, twitchy jerky movement and a faint sickly green glow.
- Homunculi come sooner: crawlers 1:00, stumblers 1:45, bone piles 2:30, twin-skulls 3:15, first Abomination 4:00 and one more every minute.
- Legs are tougher: 50% more detach resistance and 3 crush HP.
- Crawling: legless skeletons can no longer jump. Hold Space for 2.5 seconds to scoop up a dirt ball, then throw it like a limb. It staggers whoever it hits.
- Online play fixes: clearer error messages, a 60 second wait for the sleeping server, a connection log (Open log folder on the Join screen), and a fix for the freezes and crashes when closing the game.
- New kill feed lines for fireworks, bombs, swords and dirt balls.
