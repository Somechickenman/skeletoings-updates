Update 0.2.3
- NEW: a skeleton-hand cursor that closes into a fist when you click and twitches over buttons.
- NEW: a staged start screen: a Library throne room, a Graveyard picnic and a Pirate ship at sea, picked at random, plus rotating tips and a new intro.
- NEW: spectating. After you die, keep watching; or join a match already in progress as a spectator. Nametags above players (can be turned off).
- NEW: a redesigned Customize screen with collectible cards, a colour row, style slots with padlocks, and a 3D dice that shakes up a random outfit. Rattle a padlock 50 times for a secret achievement.
- NEW: a shopkeeper skeleton runs the shop. Items are cards with price tags, daily packs are crates, and he has opinions about everything you hover over.
- NEW: tarot card style panels and buttons across the whole game, a redesigned achievements page and patch notes.
- Skeleton heads have real eye sockets and a nose now, and cards and lobby portraits show a real skeleton wearing your look.
- Beheaded skeletons keep walking: the body staggers toward its skull while the skull rolls back to it. The body picks the head up when they meet.
- Smarter AI: armless AIs go looking for arms and keep fighting, AIs fetch swords and shields, and they get unstuck from walls and counters instead of sitting there.
- The spawn menu now only exists in the Sandbox.
- Numbers 5, 7 and 8 are readable now. Menu sounds are always the same sound instead of random ones.
- Fixed a ghost outline when spinning the skeleton in Customize, the skeleton starting backwards there, and the bandana poking through the skull.
- Fixed dying sending you back to the start screen instead of staying in your party.

Update 0.2.2
- NEW MAP: the Library. Walls of books, aisles, reading tables, books you can grab and throw, and chandeliers that creak, then fall.
- Teeth and achievements: 21 achievements (some secret) pay out Teeth. They only count in real matches, never the sandbox.

- Six new cosmetics: Dead Saint, Head Chef, Funny Bone, Rotor Skull, Eyes On You, Bone Appetit.

- The Pit and Pillar Garden were rebuilt: a skull monument, tiered stands, ribs, ruins and slopes. The Ship, Castle and Graveyard got a lot of scenery.
- Solid stands, and anything knocked over the rim is out of the ring. Lots of floating bits fixed.
- Ship cannons now matter: wider aim, shells explode on impact, recoil, and a splash in the sea.
- Swords, shields, rockets, bombs and dirt balls have proper textures. Sounds are now real recordings.
- Emotes: the salute is replaced by Heart, plus new Clap, Dance and Shrug.
- Digging a dirt ball takes 2 seconds and looks like digging. Legless sideways rolls are real barrel rolls.
- Fixed grabbing the same item with both hands by accident.

Update 0.2.1
- New "Test my connection" button on the Join screen: it checks the server, your firewall/UDP and your router and tells you what is blocking online play.
- Better connection logs (Open log folder), including what both sides see while connecting.

Update 0.2.0
- NEW MAP: the Castle. An indoor hall and an outdoor courtyard joined by doorways.
- Swords (one hand, long reach, knock bones loose easily but barely crush them) in the hall and the courtyard.
- Shields (one hand). Hold F + M1/M2 to block: the shield soaks the hit, the arm takes no damage. Shields wear out and break.
- Firework stand in the courtyard (new rockets appear on a timer). F near one, or while holding one, lights a 3 second fuse. R + M1/M2 grabs it, M1 + M2 grips it with both hands.
- Ride a rocket: hold it when the fuse ends and it carries you where you point for 6 seconds, then explodes. R lets go mid-flight. A rocket nobody holds shoots off in a straight line and explodes on the first thing it hits.
- Pirate ship: cannonballs are the bombs now. A landed cannonball sits on the deck as an inert bomb. Pick it up (R) to light a 4 second fuse; throw it or drop it, the fuse keeps burning. Sparking fuse, flashing bomb and a shrinking ring show the countdown. The old loose bombs are gone.
- Homunculi are cursed now: wrong sockets, wrong proportions, an arm where the head should be, twitchy jerky movement and a faint sickly green glow.
- Homunculi come sooner: crawlers 1:00, stumblers 1:45, bone piles 2:30, twin-skulls 3:15, first Abomination 4:00 and one more every minute.
- Legs are tougher: 50% more detach resistance and 3 crush HP.
- Crawling: legless skeletons can no longer jump. Hold Space for 2.5 seconds to scoop up a dirt ball, then throw it like a limb. It staggers whoever it hits.
- Online play fixes: clearer error messages, a 60 second wait for the sleeping server, a connection log (Open log folder on the Join screen), and a fix for the freezes and crashes when closing the game.
- New kill feed lines for fireworks, bombs, swords and dirt balls.
